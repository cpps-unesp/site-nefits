On March 11, 2026, researcher Roan Matthaeus Chimello Dias completed his master's degree with the defense of his dissertation entitled 'Alienation as a Dimension of Poverty?: The Economic and Philosophical Manuscripts as a Critique of Impoverishment.'

The examining committee consisted of Professors Hélio Alexandre da Silva (UNESP), Leonardo da Hora (UFBA), Pedro Geraldo Aparecido Novelli (UNESP), and Vinícius Santos (UFBA).

The group congratulates the researcher on his excellent work and approval!
