In the second half of 2025, the group organized a series of meetings with international scholars to strengthen institutional ties and foster the internationalization of its research.

The program commenced on August 13 with the participation of Professor Diana Marcela Gomes Correal, Ph.D., former Vice-Minister for Women of Colombia, who delivered a lecture titled 'Los feminismos descoloniales en Améfrica Ladina: debates y contribuciones para el pensamiento, la política y el cambio social en Latinoamerica'.

On September 10, the discussions continued with Professor Ernest Mbonda, Ph.D., from the Catholic University of Central Africa / Yaoundé, Cameroon, who addressed the theme 'African Philosophy: Ubuntu and Human Rights'.

The series concluded on November 5 with a meeting led by Professor Thierry Ngosso, Ph.D., from the Catholic University of Central Africa and the University of Maroua, Cameroon, who facilitated a debate on 'Reconceptualizing Sustainable Development and its Ethical Implications'.
